# Request for Proposal (Example/Illustrative)
## Marketing Efficiency, Cost-per-Campaign Reduction & Expense Ratio Impact Platform

GEICO has not issued a public RFP on this topic; this draft is constructed by projecting a plausible RFP from publicly available evidence about GEICO's marketing organization, financials, and parent-company (Berkshire Hathaway) priorities. **Issuing organization (illustrative):** GEICO Marketing Operations / Finance
**RFP focus area:** Problem #3 of 5 identified in prior research — cost-per-campaign and marketing-expense efficiency, under active profitability pressure
**Prepared:** 2026-08-29

## 1. Background and Context

GEICO's underwriting profitability weakened in 2025 even as premium volume grew: pretax underwriting earnings fell from $7.8B (2024) to $6.8B (2025), and the combined ratio (a measure of underwriting cost efficiency, where lower is better) worsened from 81.5% to 84.7% as losses and loss-adjustment expenses rose 6% to $32.1B. [Source: RepairerDrivenNews, March 4, 2026, https://www.repairerdrivennews.com/2026/03/04/new-ceo-of-geico-parent-company-says-it-will-write-less-pc-business/]

At the same time, GEICO's advertising spend appears to be rebounding after a deep 2021–2023 pullback (spend fell from ~$2.07B in 2021 to $838.2M in 2023, roughly a 60% cumulative cut), coinciding with the company's "largest creative push ever" in 2025. [Source: businessmodelanalyst.com, undated; MediaPost, August 15, 2025, https://www.mediapost.com/publications/article/408204/geico-discusses-its-largest-ever-creative-rollou.html]

Berkshire Hathaway's new CEO, Greg Abel, has publicly signaled that GEICO (and Berkshire's P&C operations generally) will likely "write less business for a period of time," explicitly citing continued investment in "technology to improve efficiency and service, while preserving its position as the industry's low-cost provider." [Source: RepairerDrivenNews, March 4, 2026] Separately, Berkshire Vice Chair Ajit Jain has publicly credited GEICO's expense-ratio advantage ("lower costs than virtually anybody," expense ratio below 10%) as GEICO's "fundamental advantage" over competitors such as Progressive. [Source: Carrier Management, May 6, 2024, https://www.carriermanagement.com/news/2024/05/06/261800.htm]

This combination — rising marketing spend, worsening underwriting margins, and a parent company whose stated identity is cost discipline rather than growth-at-any-cost — means marketing efficiency is likely to face materially higher internal scrutiny in the near term.

## 2. Purpose and Objectives

GEICO Marketing Operations and Finance seek proposals from qualified vendors for a platform capable of measurably reducing cost-per-campaign and cost-per-asset across GEICO's marketing production and distribution pipeline, with defensible reporting suitable for internal expense-ratio and ROI review. The objective is to:

1. Reduce the fully-loaded cost of producing and launching a marketing campaign (internal labor, agency hours, rework/revision cycles, and tooling overhead).
2. Provide finance-grade, auditable reporting on marketing operational spend tied to specific campaigns and cost centers — reporting that can plausibly be referenced in expense-ratio-adjacent internal reviews.
3. Reduce cycle time from campaign brief to launch, on the reasoning that shorter cycles reduce billable agency hours and internal labor cost per campaign.
4. Demonstrate cost efficiency gains without compromising the volume or quality of concurrent multi-line campaign output (see companion RFP for the volume/process requirements this must not undermine).

## 3. Scope of Work

### 3.1 Core capabilities required
- Cost tracking at the campaign and asset level, capturing internal labor time, external agency billable hours (where visible), tooling/licensing costs, and rework/revision costs.
- Automated identification of high-cost, high-friction stages in the production process (e.g., excessive revision rounds, redundant approvals, idle time between hand-offs).
- Reporting dashboards suitable for both marketing leadership and finance stakeholders, distinguishing "cost of production" from "cost of media" (GEICO's media spend is managed separately through its media agency of record, IPG Mediabrands).
- Benchmarking capability — cost and cycle-time trends over time, by campaign type and product line, to demonstrate improvement (or regression) quarter over quarter.
- Support for scenario modeling: e.g., projected cost impact of adding or removing a creative-agency partner, or of increasing campaign volume by a given percentage.

### 3.2 Out of scope (for this RFP)
- Media buying and placement cost optimization (outside marketing operations' direct scope; owned by the media agency of record relationship).
- Underwriting or claims-side expense-ratio components (this RFP addresses only the marketing/advertising expense line).

## 6. Grounding & Rationale (why each requirement exists, and why now)

**The core argument, stated as a chain of reasoning:**

1. **Fact:** GEICO's combined ratio worsened from 81.5% to 84.7% in 2025 — profitability is compressing. [RepairerDrivenNews, March 4, 2026]
2. **Fact:** GEICO's ad spend is simultaneously rebounding after a multi-year cut, coinciding with record creative output. [businessmodelanalyst.com; MediaPost, August 15, 2025]
3. **Inference (high confidence):** When profitability is compressing at the same time a discretionary cost line (marketing) is rising, that line becomes a natural target for internal cost scrutiny — marketing spend is far more controllable, quarter to quarter, than claims payouts, which are largely dictated by loss events outside the company's control.
4. **Fact:** GEICO's parent company (Berkshire Hathaway) has repeatedly and publicly framed GEICO's competitive identity around cost discipline specifically — not creative output, not growth. New Berkshire CEO Greg Abel explicitly linked "investing in technology to improve efficiency" with "preserving its position as the industry's low-cost provider." [RepairerDrivenNews, March 4, 2026] Ajit Jain separately called GEICO's expense-ratio advantage its "fundamental advantage." [Carrier Management, May 6, 2024]
5. **Conclusion:** A platform pitched on cost-per-campaign and cycle-time reduction — with finance-grade, auditable reporting — speaks directly to the metric GEICO's own ownership already uses to evaluate the business, rather than requiring GEICO's marketing team to make a novel argument to finance/leadership. This is a materially stronger argument than a "creative capability" or "speed for its own sake" pitch, because it doesn't require translating the value proposition into terms finance already tracks — it already is one.

**Why finance-consumable reporting is Must-have (F3):** The pitch only works if marketing leadership can hand a number to finance/leadership that maps onto existing scrutiny (expense ratio, cost discipline) rather than a marketing-only metric finance has no frame of reference for.

**Why this must not undermine campaign volume (Section 2, objective 4):** GEICO is not retreating from marketing volume — 2025 was its largest creative push ever, and Berkshire's guidance is about writing less *underwriting* business, not necessarily cutting marketing. The efficiency argument must be "same or more output, lower cost per unit," not "produce less," which would work against GEICO's own stated growth-oriented marketing direction under its new CMO.

## 7. Illustrative ROI Benchmarks (industry-wide, not GEICO-specific)

Cited for realistic cost/efficiency framing — vendor-commissioned studies, not GEICO-confirmed figures:

- **Wrike** (GEICO's most plausibly current marketing-ops tool, per public job-posting evidence): Forrester TEI study (August 23, 2023) found a composite customer achieved **396% ROI over 3 years**, **$9.78M net present value**, **payback within 6 months**, and a **90% reduction in low-value initiatives** — directly relevant if GEICO's actual environment is confirmed to be Wrike-based, since incremental efficiency gains would layer on top of an already-deployed tool rather than requiring a full replacement. [https://www.wrike.com/blog/forrester-tei-study-2023/]
- **Adobe Workfront** (comparator): Forrester TEI study found **285% ROI over 3 years**, **$22M+ in productivity savings**, **payback under 3 months**, including **$4.26M in savings from decreased cycle times** and **$267,971 annually** from eliminating status meetings and administrative overhead in one composite case. [https://business.adobe.com/blog/the-latest/forrester-how-enterprises-hit-285-roi]

**How to use these numbers responsibly:** Both are vendor-funded studies (Forrester TEI methodology), so treat the specific percentages as directionally realistic, not as a promise. The pattern worth borrowing for a GEICO-specific pitch is the *category* of saving — cycle-time reduction, meeting/administrative-overhead elimination, and reduced "low-value" rework — not the exact percentage. Any GEICO-specific dollar estimate used in a demo should be explicitly labeled as illustrative, built from GEICO's own likely order-of-magnitude (large-enterprise, multi-billion-dollar premium base) rather than lifted directly from either study.

## 8. Submission Requirements (illustrative)

Respondents should provide:
1. Executive summary framed around cost-per-campaign and expense-ratio-adjacent impact, not general marketing capability.
2. Quantified case studies showing before/after cost-per-campaign or cost-per-asset figures, ideally from regulated or large-scale consumer enterprises.
3. Description of finance-facing reporting outputs, with sample dashboards or exports.
4. Three-year total cost of ownership model with projected payback period, using conservative (not best-case) assumptions.
5. Integration plan with existing marketing tooling (Wrike, pending confirmation).
6. Named references who can speak specifically to cost/efficiency outcomes, not general satisfaction.
